Health MCP

A personal, single-user connector for the Google Health API.

Health MCP is a private tool that reads its owner's own Google Health data — steps, sleep, heart rate and related metrics recorded by a Fitbit device — and makes it available to the owner's local software for personal tracking.

It runs entirely on the owner's own computer. It is not a product, it is not offered to other users, and it is not intended for anyone else to sign in to.
